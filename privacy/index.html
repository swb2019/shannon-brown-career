Privacy

A small site.
A clear policy.

Effective 6 September 2026.

What this portfolio does

This site does not use analytics, advertising trackers, visitor profiling, contact forms, or account registration. Fonts and visual assets are served with the site. It does not access your microphone or load Hourglass Command in the background.

Your motion preference

If you change the motion setting, the site attempts to save that preference in your browser’s local storage under sb-motion. That value is not sent to Shannon. You can clear it through your browser’s site-data controls. The site remains usable when browser storage is unavailable.

Hosting and external links

The public portfolio is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data under its privacy statement. A hosted copy may also be served through ChatGPT Sites, whose platform policies apply to that copy. Following LinkedIn, GitHub, source-reference, or simulation links takes you to a separate service with its own data practices.

Contact

Email links open your chosen mail application. If you email Shannon, the contents and address you provide are handled as ordinary correspondence. For questions about this site, use shannon@i-mail.se.
